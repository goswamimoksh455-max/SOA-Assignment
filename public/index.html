SOA Design Specifications
Architectural standards implemented in this system
Standard
  • Loose Coupling & Domain Cohesion
    Separated concerns across controllers, repositories, validation, and serialization. Modules function independently through standardized REST interfaces.
  • Double-Booking Collision Prevention Engine
    Autonomous slot conflict engine enforces integrity constraints at the domain boundary, returning HTTP 409 Conflict when slot collisions occur.
  • Master Patient Index (PMI) & Longitudinal EHR
    Centralized patient directory collating cross-service timeline data: consultation logs, issued medications, and diagnostic investigations.
  • Laboratory Information Service (LIS)
    Decoupled diagnostic workflow for specimen lifecycle tracking, turnaround metrics, and automated pathology interpretation flags (NORMAL, ABNORMAL, CRITICAL).
  • State-Machine Appointment Transitions
    Strict lifecycle transitions: CONFIRMED → RESCHEDULED → COMPLETED (triggered upon prescription delivery).
